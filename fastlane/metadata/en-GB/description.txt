Screen time control for the whole family.

Block distracting apps with a tap, a schedule, or a physical NFC scan - making the choice to unblock deliberate, not automatic.

BUILT FOR FAMILIES
Parents create a Family Lock Code and manage settings. Kids set up their own profiles and activate focus modes freely - but can't change the rules. Only parents can unlock and edit locked profiles.

BLOCK WHAT STEALS YOUR ATTENTION
TikTok, Instagram, Snapchat, games - choose the apps that pull you in and block them your way. Schedule blocks ahead of time, toggle them instantly, or require a physical trigger to stay accountable.

PHYSICAL TRIGGERS, INTENTIONAL CHOICES
Tap an NFC tag or scan a QR code to unblock. Adding friction to the moment makes mindless scrolling harder and deliberate focus easier.

SYNC BLOCKS ACROSS DEVICES
Stay accountable to yourself - once set up, blocks can be synced across devices so that you can't cheat by switching to your iPad.

Based on the open-source Foqos app, with added support for lockable profiles and family PIN syncing.
